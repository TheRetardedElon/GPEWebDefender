Non-negotiable design truths

TruthImplication
Out of band We tail logs. We do not proxy HTTP. A crash here cannot take the site down.
Access logs are the sensor URL, method, status, UA, IP. Not POST bodies. Do not start logging card fields to “see more.”
No Java pile One Go binary + SQLite. We do not add OpenSearch, Elasticsearch, or Wazuh “just for search.”
Not OSSEC source We stole the job (see web attacks cheaply). We did not fork OSSEC or Wazuh.
Demo is fake gpewebdefender demo invents lines. It never talks to your site.
Bind localhost until you mean it Default listen is 127.0.0.1:8787. Do not put the UI on the public internet without a token and a gate.
